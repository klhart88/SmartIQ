The SmartIQ guides

Three guides. Three starting points. All focused on clarity.

Choose the guide that fits where you are right now. Each one is designed to slow the process down so the next step becomes obvious.

01
First-time buyer

First-Time Buyer
Roadmap

Buying your first home should not feel like guessing. This guide helps you think through money, lifestyle, timing, and the steps from pre-approval to closing — at your own pace, without the pressure.

  • Understand what affordability really means before you search
  • Clear the most common first-buyer money concerns — down payments, credit, student loans
  • Learn what pre-approval actually requires and why it comes first
  • Follow the path from offer to closing, step by step

Experienced buyer

Buy Smart:
Your Next Home

Buying again can feel familiar — but every move has a different financial picture, timeline, and set of tradeoffs. This guide helps experienced buyers reset assumptions, confirm the numbers, and make a smarter next-home decision.

  • Refresh your affordability picture for this season, not the last one
  • Understand why pre-approval still comes before showings
  • Define the tradeoffs — location, condition, maintenance, future flexibility
  • Avoid the mistakes that create stress after closing

03
Seller

Sell Smart:
Your Next Move

Selling well is not just putting a sign in the yard. This guide helps you prepare the home, understand your equity, price with strategy, and move forward with a plan that keeps the sale on track from prep to closing.

  • Prepare the home so buyers see the space, not the distractions
  • Understand your equity and how it connects to the next move
  • Price with strategy — not just hope — and protect first-week momentum
  • Know what to watch for from contract to closing

The person behind SmartIQ

Kelvin Hart built these guides because buying and selling should feel like a plan, not a gamble.

Kelvin is a Realtor® with Fathom Realty Indiana serving buyers and sellers across the Indianapolis area. He created the SmartIQ brand — including these guides, AffordabilityIQ, and AreaIQ — because he believes that informed clients make calmer, better decisions.

The guides are free. The tools are free. The goal is simple: give you a clearer place to start so that when you are ready to talk, the conversation is more productive and less overwhelming.

When you are ready to take the next step, Kelvin can help you connect what you learned here to your actual situation — your numbers, your home, your timing.
